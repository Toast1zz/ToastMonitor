## New

- Antigravity support. ToastMonitor now counts the tokens Antigravity uses, whether you run it on its own or inside the Gemini app, with each conversation's title and project. Costs are estimated from Google's published Gemini prices; the Gemini 4 models have no public price yet.
- Antigravity quota in the panel and on Plans. Gemini shows its 5-hour and weekly limits, like Claude. Argon and the other models (Claude, GPT-OSS) each get one line once you have used them. Quota is read from Antigravity on this Mac, so Antigravity or the Gemini app must be running to refresh it. The last reading stays on screen while it is closed, and a limit counts as full again once its reset time passes.
- Google AI is a plan option in Subscriptions. A Google AI plan names the Antigravity row.
- Performance includes Antigravity: speed and time to first token for each Gemini model.

Chats in the Gemini app itself are not counted, because the app keeps no usage record on your Mac.
